## Authority and API

The supervisor holds one immutable request, accepted coordinator and owned
driver. Its one non-daemon thread serializes coordinator start/observe and
driver close. Concurrent callers can set durable cancellation intent through
the store and read owned status; they cannot call the driver. `start` is one
shot even after timeout, failure or close. Bounded `start`/`wait` use local
snapshots after their event waits; `status` explicitly performs the DB read.
`close` signals stop and uses a bounded join; on timeout or failed driver
cleanup the same owner thread and driver handle remain available for a later
close retry. No process is selected or killed by a persisted PID.

The owner polls the accepted coordinator within a validated fraction of its
lease and consumes cancellation intent on the owner thread. A close before
start prevents launch. Unknown/absent observations, owner expiry, failed DB
acknowledgement and failed active operations retain the accepted conservative
uncertain fence. Cleanup does not manufacture a terminal receipt or release a
budget. Exact terminal receipts can be recovered through the accepted
coordinator/store without another launch. Cleanup failure remains separately
visible even after a durable terminal result.

## Limits for Main review

The caller's explicit `request_cancel` and `status` make synchronous store calls;
their latency depends on the trusted connection factory and accepted store
timeouts. `start`/`wait` do not add DB work after the bounded wait, and `close`
only signals the owner plus a bounded join. The declared driver call budget is
validated against the lease but cannot preempt a blocking injected driver.
Scheduling pauses or slow connection setup can still expire the lease and leave
uncertainty. A failed cleanup retains a non-daemon thread until a later close
retry succeeds; operators must retain the supervisor object and complete that
cleanup. Full native runtime plus PostgreSQL qualification, process lifetime
operations, checkpointing, paid-provider dispatch, accounting and whole U05
acceptance remain outside this packet.
