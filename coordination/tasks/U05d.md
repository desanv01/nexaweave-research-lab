## Outcome

Connect accepted NativeRunCoordinator and NativeProcessDriver through a trusted
host-owned supervision loop so callers need not manually poll to maintain the
lease. One supervisor instance owns at most one request/attempt and one driver;
it never restarts, retries fresh start, adopts a persisted PID, transfers an
owner lease, clears a fresh-start marker or releases budget. This is a reusable
host seam, not an HTTP endpoint, Temporal retry policy or completed application.

Use a single background owner thread to serialize coordinator start/observe/
cancel and driver cleanup. Only this thread calls the driver while active.
Concurrent callers may request durable cancellation and obtain status through
the store, but must not race a second driver invocation. Bound principal and
request immutably; dispatch remains explicit default OFF through the accepted
coordinator. Avoid eager imports of native SDKs or Temporal. No provider creation.

Expose a small documented start/wait/status/request_cancel/close API with finite
caller wait bounds. Worker may choose names/return types appropriate to accepted
contracts. A caller timeout is not proof of failed start and never grants another
launch. Permanent one-start rule also applies to concurrent/repeated calls and
shutdown. Thread and child may still exist after a bounded close times out;
retain cleanup authority and allow a later close to retry. Never claim terminated
from cancellation intent alone. Do not signal a process by stored PID.

Poll the coordinator often enough to extend its DB-clock lease while the actual
child runs. Validate finite positive polling/wait/shutdown values (reject bool,
NaN and infinity); enforce a conservative relationship to coordinator lease.
Document the trusted driver/store call bounds and scheduling assumptions: an
arbitrary injected blocking driver is not made safe by a thread. Existing store
transaction and connection boundaries apply. Do not hold local locks across
external driver/database calls; do not hold DB transactions across work or wait.

On stop request, consume cancellation through the active owner, then perform
bounded cleanup of the same owned driver. If owner lease expires, observations
are absent/unknown, start/attach/settlement loses acknowledgement, or DB operation
fails, retain the conservative uncertain durable fence; clean only the locally
owned handle. Never manufacture a success/cancelled receipt from close, errors,
thread completion or an OS exit. Exact terminal records recover through accepted
coordinator/store without another launch. Cleanup failure must remain visible
and retryable independently of an already durable terminal result. Expose fixed
safe local status/errors, never exception strings, artifact paths or secrets.

Handle cancellation during launch, shutdown before first go, stop racing terminal
receipt and failure during cleanup. Preserve KeyboardInterrupt/SystemExit cleanup
semantics when applicable; no unbounded thread join or daemon-only cleanup claim.
No new process-global signal handlers, atexit hooks or global event loops.
